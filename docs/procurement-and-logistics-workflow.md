# Procurement and logistics workflow

Procurement is implemented through confirmed Purchase Orders. Proforma
Invoices, payable schedules, supplier payments, Production Orders, technical
and sample approvals, quality reports, and packing records are present.

Logistics is `PARTIAL`: Shipment creation supports partial quantities, PO and
Production readiness checks, manual tracking, lifecycle history, documents,
Customs declarations with backend tax totals, and Decimal landed-cost
allocation/finalization. Real list/detail/create UI and project-filtered
Shipment views are present. Direct authorization/E2E coverage, complete
Freight Quotation UI, and full customs/import-tax document commands remain.
No carrier or customs provider integration is claimed.
